How to play

Quick notes for your first run.

  • Click + drag near the cue ball to aim and set power.
  • Release to shoot. Longer drag gives a stronger hit.
  • Clear the target score before you run out of shots.
  • Winning a round opens the shop. Spend credits on upgrades.
  • Boss rounds add a table modifier, so adjust your shot choices.
  • While balls are moving, click once to skip to the shot result.
